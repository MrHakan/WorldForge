# WorldForge v4.9 — Warfare & Supply Lines

v4.9 connects warfare directly to the living economy, infrastructure and diplomacy layers introduced in v4.5–v4.8 and v2.9–v3.0.

## Implemented

- Field armies generated from sufficiently populated settlements
- Army size, supply, food, mobility and attrition states
- Persistent food reserves and yearly consumption / resupply
- Explicit ammunition reserve and equipment readiness derived from iron, textiles, throughput and route reliability
- Morale, experience and composite combat-power values
- Deterministic hostile target selection
- True marching state with progress, speed and target city
- Realm-level mobilization laws: Peacetime Levy, Limited Mobilization, General Mobilization and Emergency Levy
- Mobilization pressure responds to casualties, sieges, readiness and available manpower
- Mobilization modifies activated manpower, recovery, reinforcement priority and army morale
- Weighted military route graph built from settlement coordinates, road quality and biome/terrain penalties
- Dijkstra shortest-path routing between army origin and target settlements
- Army positions now follow multi-node graph routes instead of direct origin-to-target interpolation
- Route-derived march cost and march speed react to road quality and terrain difficulty
- Dedicated military supply lines inherit route nodes, path cost, road quality and terrain penalties
- Supply-line reliability now incorporates route quality in addition to existing logistics state
- Warfare overlay renders routed supply paths and exposes route node / quality metadata
- Dedicated military supply-line model and map overlay, separate from civilian trade routes
- Ready / strained / critical readiness states now react to both logistics and morale
- Supply-line reliability and open / fragile / broken states
- Persistent settlement manpower pools with capacity, yearly recovery and recruitment draw
- Recruitment only tops forces back toward desired strength rather than re-recruiting the whole army every year
- Casualties from attrition and battle reduce field strength and local manpower
- Siege activation only once armies approach their target
- Siege pressure influenced by food, ammunition, equipment, morale, mobility and target fortification
- Fortification modifier derived from gates, walls, water and roads
- Relief armies are selected from defending realms and can trigger siege-breaking battles
- Deterministic attacker/defender casualties, morale shifts, veteran experience and siege-broken / relief-repelled outcomes
- Coastal settlements create fleet formations from dock, port and logistics capacity
- Fleets receive deterministic patrol, raid, intercept and retreat missions
- Fleet movement persists between yearly simulation steps with progress, speed, target fleet/city and map position
- Hostile fleets can intercept one another when their operational envelopes overlap
- Naval battles apply strength and supply losses, battle-damaged / crippled states and retreat behavior
- Realm sea-control shares are derived from surviving fleet strength and supply readiness
- Naval battle history, underway fleets and active interception counts are exposed through the Warfare API
- Fleet strength and supply continue to contribute naval support to coastal sieges
- Persistent blockade pressure disrupts v4.8 sea trade routes through the economy disruption API
- v2.9 diplomacy / history active-war state is now authoritative for hostile military operations
- Armies only retain or acquire targets belonging to realms with an active war relationship
- Armies are deterministically retargeted to the nearest settlement of an active enemy realm when a previous target becomes invalid
- Peace immediately cancels invalid marches, supply lines, sieges and relief battles and returns affected armies to garrison state
- Fleets cannot continue raid / interception targets against realms that are no longer at war
- Invalid naval battle records for the current warfare state are removed and fleets return to patrol after peace
- Warfare exposes active-war, retargeting, peaceful-garrison and demobilization metrics
- Settlement military views expose current war state and enemy realm IDs
- Warfare & Supply workspace page under Realms
- Incoming army, march progress, manpower, casualties, ammunition, equipment, morale, experience, relief battle, fleet mission, naval battle and war-state inspection
- Warfare map overlay includes routed supply lines, army markers, fleet movement vectors, fleet mission metadata and naval battle markers
- CI contract coverage for engine, mobilization, pathfinding, naval warfare, diplomacy integration, UI, overlay and loader integration
