# WorldForge v4.9 — Warfare & Supply Lines

v4.9 connects warfare directly to the living economy and infrastructure layers introduced in v4.5–v4.8.

## Implemented first slice

- Field armies generated from sufficiently populated settlements
- Army size, supply, food, mobility and attrition states
- Supply derived from v4.8 food security, grain stockpiles, logistics throughput and route reliability
- Mobility derived from roads, caravan capacity and active trade-route reliability
- Ready / strained / critical army readiness states
- Deterministic siege target selection
- Siege pressure influenced by army supply, mobility, city gates, water infrastructure and shortage pressure
- Coastal siege blockades can disrupt v4.8 sea trade routes through the economy disruption API
- Warfare & Supply workspace page under Realms
- Selected settlement military and siege inspection
