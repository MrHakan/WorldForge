# WorldForge v5.0 — Strategic AI & War Consequences

v5.0 turns v4.9 warfare into a persistent strategic campaign layer while keeping warfare, diplomacy, logistics and peace systems authoritative.

## Implemented milestones

### Strategic AI Core
Active wars generate deterministic fronts, army assignments, campaign objectives and siege-derived occupation control.

### Commanders & Leadership
Persistent commanders have skill, seniority, prestige and situational traits with bounded combat effects and retirement history.

### Strategic Reserves & Reinforcements
Front collapse risk creates reinforcement requests; suitable reserve armies are ranked and committed with fulfillment tracking.

### Multi-Year Campaign Planning & Objective Reassessment
Campaigns persist momentum, posture, phase, priority and reassessment history across years. Failed offensives can fall back to hold-line while recovered campaigns can resume territorial objectives.

### Occupation Policy, Resistance, Collaboration & Liberation
- Occupied settlements now receive persistent occupation-administration state rather than only siege-control metadata.
- Policies include `military-rule`, `conciliatory`, `collaboration` and `extraction`.
- Policy selection begins from resistance and administrative-control conditions and then persists across occupation years.
- Resistance, collaboration and administrative control evolve annually according to policy and occupation pressure.
- Military rule improves control quickly but tends to increase resistance; conciliatory administration reduces resistance; collaboration policy builds local collaboration; extraction raises control and resistance together.
- Occupations expose `yearsOccupied`, resistance status, collaboration-government state and liberation risk.
- When an occupation disappears from the authoritative Strategic AI occupation list, its final state is preserved as a liberated historical record.
- Warfare summaries expose occupied policy states, high-resistance occupations, collaboration governments and newly liberated settlements.
- Settlement military views expose the local occupation-policy state.
- Dedicated Occupation & Resistance UI shows policy, control, resistance, collaboration, liberation risk and duration.

## Architecture

The strategic modules wrap `WorldForgeWarfareSupply`; they do not create a second combat engine. v4.9 remains authoritative for armies, supply, sieges, naval warfare, diplomacy, post-war recovery and peace terms. v5.0 derives strategic planning and consequences from those systems.
