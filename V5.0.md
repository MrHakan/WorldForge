# WorldForge v5.0 — Strategic AI & War Consequences

v5.0 turns v4.9 warfare into a persistent strategic campaign layer while keeping warfare, diplomacy, logistics and peace systems authoritative.

## Implemented milestones

### Strategic AI Core
Active wars generate deterministic fronts, army assignments, campaign objectives and siege-derived occupation control.

### Commanders & Leadership
Persistent commanders have skill, seniority, prestige and situational traits with bounded combat effects and retirement history.

### Strategic Reserves & Reinforcements
Front collapse risk creates reinforcement requests; suitable reserve armies are ranked and committed with fulfillment tracking.

### Multi-Year Campaign Planning & Objective Reassessment
Campaigns persist momentum, posture, phase, priority and reassessment history across years. Failed offensives can fall back to hold-line while recovered campaigns can resume territorial objectives.

### Occupation Policy, Resistance, Collaboration & Liberation
Occupation policies, resistance, collaboration, administrative control, liberation risk and liberated-history persistence are simulated annually.

### Front-line Visualization & Campaign Arrows
- A dedicated non-interactive SVG strategic overlay renders beneath the existing clickable army/fleet overlay.
- Fronts connect attacker and defender anchor settlements with status-specific line patterns.
- Contested fronts receive expanded dashed pressure rings; collapse risk affects front-zone radius.
- Campaign plans render curved objective arrows toward their current target settlement.
- Arrow weight and dash style reflect campaign momentum, phase and posture.
- Strategic reserve commitments render reinforcement arrows from reserve home settlements to the threatened front anchor.
- Occupied settlements render hatched control zones scaled by liberation risk and resistance.
- Overlay elements expose semantic data attributes for front status, campaign phase/posture/momentum, reinforcement priority and occupation policy/resistance.
- `pointer-events:none` preserves existing army marker selection and map interaction behavior.
- The layer refreshes with warfare revisions, simulation-year changes, resize and visibility return.

## Architecture

The strategic modules wrap `WorldForgeWarfareSupply`; they do not create a second combat engine. v4.9 remains authoritative for armies, supply, sieges, naval warfare, diplomacy, post-war recovery and peace terms. v5.0 derives strategic planning, consequences and map visualization from those systems.
