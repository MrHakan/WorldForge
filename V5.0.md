# WorldForge v5.0 — Strategic AI & War Consequences

v5.0 begins turning the v4.9 warfare simulation into a strategic campaign layer. The first milestones add fronts, campaign objectives, occupations, persistent commanders, strategic reserves and multi-year campaign planning without replacing the existing warfare, diplomacy, logistics or peace systems.

## Implemented — Strategic AI Core

- Active wars generate one to three deterministic fronts from the nearest opposing settlement pairs.
- Fronts track attacker/defender anchor settlements, assigned field armies, combat power balance and contested/advantage state.
- Armies receive persistent `frontId` and `strategicRole` metadata (`offensive`, `defensive`, `reserve`).
- Each active war generates a campaign plan derived from its existing v4.9 war goal.
- Campaign objective types include capture-capital, seize-border, break-army, liberate-settlement and hold-line.
- Conquest goals prioritize the defender's largest settlement; defensive goals anchor on the threatened friendly front.
- Campaigns expose target settlement, priority, front assignment and active status.
- High-pressure sieges produce occupation-control state with occupier, original realm, controlling army, administrative control and resistance pressure.
- Strategic summary metrics expose active fronts, campaigns, contested fronts and occupied settlements.
- Settlement military views expose associated fronts and occupation state.
- Dedicated Strategic AI & Fronts UI provides a campaign-planning inspection surface.

## Implemented — Commanders & Leadership

- Every active field army receives a deterministic persistent commander identity.
- Commanders track realm, assigned army, command skill, seniority, battles, prestige and service status.
- Leadership traits include aggressive, cautious, logistician, siege specialist, defender, inspirational and naval-minded.
- Command skill and seniority provide bounded combat modifiers rather than replacing the underlying combat-power model.
- Traits gain situational bonuses from offensive/defensive strategic roles, supply stress, siege duty and morale leadership.
- Army state exposes `commanderId`, `commanderTrait`, `commanderSkill` and `commandModifier`.
- Inspirational commanders can raise morale while high-skill leaders improve effective combat power.
- Long-serving commanders can retire deterministically; retired leaders remain in bounded historical records.
- Warfare summaries expose active commanders, veteran commanders, retirements and average command skill.
- Strategic AI UI includes commander and veteran KPIs plus a commander roster with army, skill, seniority and prestige.

## Implemented — Strategic Reserves & Reinforcements

- Fronts derive deterministic `collapseRisk` and reinforcement priority from combat-power imbalance and contested state.
- Weak attacker or defender sides create reinforcement requests with requested combat power and critical/requested status.
- Reserve armies are filtered by readiness, supply, realm ownership and current commitment.
- Reinforcement candidates are ranked by combat power, supply, morale, commander modifier and request priority.
- Selected reserve armies transition to `reinforcing` strategic role and expose `reinforcementFrontId` and `reinforcementPriority`.
- Requests track desired power, committed power and fulfillment percentage.
- Front state exposes reinforcement request IDs, priority, collapse risk and fulfillment status.
- Strategic reserve metrics expose open requests, critical requests, reserves available, reserves committed and high-collapse-risk fronts.
- Dedicated Strategic Reserves UI exposes request priority, collapse risk, requested power and fulfillment.

## Implemented — Multi-Year Campaign Planning & Objective Reassessment

- Active campaigns persist strategic planning state across simulation years instead of being treated as isolated annual snapshots.
- Each campaign tracks momentum, posture, phase, priority, years active and last reassessment year.
- Campaign momentum blends current front balance, reinforcement fulfillment, collapse risk and prior-year momentum.
- Campaign phases include advancing, contested, stalled and retreating.
- Strategic posture automatically shifts between offensive, balanced and defensive as momentum changes.
- A collapsing offensive can downgrade to `hold-line`; a recovered defensive campaign can exploit momentum and resume a territorial objective.
- Reassessment records why a plan changed, including momentum collapse, momentum opportunity and target changes.
- Closed wars move their final planning state into bounded campaign history.
- Warfare summaries expose advancing, stalled, retreating and reassessed campaign counts.
- Dedicated Campaign Planning UI exposes momentum, posture, years active, priority and reassessment reason.

## Architecture

The strategic layer wraps the existing `WorldForgeWarfareSupply` API. It does not create a second combat engine. v4.9 remains authoritative for armies, supply, sieges, naval warfare, diplomacy, post-war recovery and peace terms; v5.0 derives higher-level strategic, leadership, reinforcement and campaign-planning state from those systems.
