# WorldForge v5.0 — Strategic AI & War Consequences

v5.0 turns v4.9 warfare into a persistent strategic campaign layer while keeping warfare, diplomacy, logistics and peace systems authoritative.

## Implemented milestones

### Strategic AI Core
Active wars generate deterministic fronts, army assignments, campaign objectives and siege-derived occupation control.

### Commanders & Leadership
Persistent commanders have skill, seniority, prestige and situational traits with bounded combat effects.

### Strategic Reserves & Reinforcements
Front collapse risk creates reinforcement requests; suitable reserve armies are ranked and committed with fulfillment tracking. Reserve assignment reconciles stale commitments, preserves same-year idempotency, avoids arbitrary balanced-front requests and includes front distance in candidate ranking.

### Multi-Front Campaign Orchestration
A single war can maintain multiple simultaneous fronts without assigning the same army to more than one front. Each front owns an independent campaign identity and primary/secondary theater role.

### Multi-Year Campaign Planning & Objective Reassessment
Campaign plans persist across simulation years with momentum, posture, objective progress, supply reliability, reinforcement state, collapse risk, failed offensives and reassessment cooldowns. Severe supply failure or collapse risk can override normal cooldowns.

### Strategic Theater Command
Theater Command scores active theaters, maintains a stable primary theater with promotion hysteresis, redirects reserves without stripping source fronts below a safety floor and creates breakthrough/exploitation records when momentum, supply and objective progress justify it.

### Operational Movement & Reinforcement Travel
Reinforcing armies now travel over weighted military paths instead of teleporting through assignment metadata. Mobility, road quality, terrain and supply affect march speed; progress, ETA, map position, attrition and interception risk persist annually. Arrivals produce idempotent reinforcement events. Breakthrough formations can advance toward real objectives and close when reached.

### Occupation Economics, Partisans & Liberation Campaigns
Occupation policy now tracks economic retention, tax/extraction yield, logistics disruption, garrison demand and shortfall. Resistance, weak control and inadequate garrisons grow partisan networks. Strong networks create deterministic sabotage events and can open persistent liberation campaigns with annual progress. Existing occupation saves migrate in place.

### Strategic Map Interaction & Operational Intel
The strategic SVG layer is interactive while preserving the existing army/fleet hit-testing layer. Fronts, campaigns and occupation zones can be clicked directly and hovered for contextual tooltips. Selected entities receive stronger map emphasis.

A shared read-only Operational Intel model projects current strategic state into front, campaign, army and occupation intelligence. The map intel panel exposes theater role and command score, campaign momentum/objective/supply state, reinforcement fulfillment, army route progress and ETA, interception risk, occupation economics, garrison gaps, partisan strength, sabotage risk and liberation pressure. Existing army markers remain selectable through the v4.9 supply overlay and feed the same intel panel.

Campaign cards can also focus their campaign on the strategic map. Front/campaign/occupation layer toggles remain independent and map interaction does not call strategic simulation mutators.

### Commander Wounds, Battlefield Death, Succession & Historical Legacies
Commander application is idempotent within a simulation year. Leadership stores base and adjusted combat power/morale separately, prestige advances only for new battles, wounds have recovery timelines, battlefield deaths trigger succession, commander IDs remain unique, and distinguished/fallen commanders can create persistent historical legacies and memorials.

### Historical Battlefields, War Scars, Archaeology & Heritage
Completed battle records persist as war scars with significance, casualties, outcome and physical condition. Important battlefields bridge additively into World Building, Archaeology and Heritage without replacing those systems.

### Battlefield Excavations, War Relics & Provenance
Archaeology-eligible battlefields create deterministic excavation contexts and relics with battlefield provenance, rarity, condition, commander links and museum/heritage eligibility.

## Architecture

The strategic modules extend `WorldForgeWarfareSupply`; they do not create a second combat engine. v4.9 remains authoritative for armies, supply, sieges, naval warfare, diplomacy, post-war recovery and peace terms.

Annual strategic mutation is coordinated through the warfare hook registry. The operational sequence is Strategic Reserves → Theater Command → Operational Movement → Campaign Planning → Occupation Policy. UI panels and Operational Intel are read-only projections of simulation state.

The strategic map uses two compatible interaction layers: the v4.9 supply overlay remains authoritative for army marker hit testing, while the v5.0 strategic overlay owns front, campaign and occupation interaction. This avoids blocking army/fleet selection while allowing strategic objects to be inspected directly.
