# WorldForge v5.0 — Strategic AI & War Consequences

v5.0 begins turning the v4.9 warfare simulation into a strategic campaign layer. The first milestones add fronts, campaign objectives, occupations and persistent commanders without replacing the existing warfare, diplomacy, logistics or peace systems.

## Implemented — Strategic AI Core

- Active wars generate one to three deterministic fronts from the nearest opposing settlement pairs.
- Fronts track attacker/defender anchor settlements, assigned field armies, combat power balance and contested/advantage state.
- Armies receive persistent `frontId` and `strategicRole` metadata (`offensive`, `defensive`, `reserve`).
- Each active war generates a campaign plan derived from its existing v4.9 war goal.
- Campaign objective types include capture-capital, seize-border, break-army, liberate-settlement and hold-line.
- Conquest goals prioritize the defender's largest settlement; defensive goals anchor on the threatened friendly front.
- Campaigns expose target settlement, priority, front assignment and active status.
- High-pressure sieges produce occupation-control state with occupier, original realm, controlling army, administrative control and resistance pressure.
- Strategic summary metrics expose active fronts, campaigns, contested fronts and occupied settlements.
- Settlement military views expose associated fronts and occupation state.
- Dedicated Strategic AI & Fronts UI provides a campaign-planning inspection surface.

## Implemented — Commanders & Leadership

- Every active field army receives a deterministic persistent commander identity.
- Commanders track realm, assigned army, command skill, seniority, battles, prestige and service status.
- Leadership traits include aggressive, cautious, logistician, siege specialist, defender, inspirational and naval-minded.
- Command skill and seniority provide bounded combat modifiers rather than replacing the underlying combat-power model.
- Traits gain situational bonuses from offensive/defensive strategic roles, supply stress, siege duty and morale leadership.
- Army state exposes `commanderId`, `commanderTrait`, `commanderSkill` and `commandModifier`.
- Inspirational commanders can raise morale while high-skill leaders improve effective combat power.
- Long-serving commanders can retire deterministically; retired leaders remain in bounded historical records.
- Warfare summaries expose active commanders, veteran commanders, retirements and average command skill.
- Strategic AI UI now includes commander and veteran KPIs plus a commander roster with army, skill, seniority and prestige.

## Architecture

The strategic layer wraps the existing `WorldForgeWarfareSupply` API. It does not create a second combat engine. v4.9 remains authoritative for armies, supply, sieges, naval warfare, diplomacy, post-war recovery and peace terms; v5.0 derives higher-level strategic and leadership state from those systems.

## Next milestones

- Multi-year campaign planning and objective reassessment.
- Occupation policy, resistance, collaboration and liberation.
- Front-line visualization and campaign arrows on the world map.
- Commander wounds, battlefield death, succession and named historical legacies.
