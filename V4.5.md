# WorldForge v4.5 — Living Cities & Urban Evolution

v4.5 extends the Pixel Civilization and worldbuilding stack with deterministic long-term urban change. Settlements are no longer represented as static collections of districts: prosperity, population, crime, conflict damage, tourism pressure and geography now influence whether neighborhoods grow, decline, fall into ruin or rebuild.

## Core systems

- Deterministic district lifecycle states: `stable`, `growing`, `declining`, `ruined`, `rebuilding`.
- District role classification for civic, commercial, productive, religious, elite residential, poor residential and general residential areas.
- Population-density estimates derived from existing city districts and settlement population.
- Urban sprawl and outside-the-walls growth for larger settlements.
- Expansion stages: contained, edge growth, suburban growth and outer-ring expansion.
- Wall-expansion pressure for cities outgrowing their historic footprint.
- Dynamic skyline score influenced by settlement size, prosperity and urban class.
- Crisis damage derived from recent siege, battle, raid, fire, plague, epidemic and famine history.
- Deterministic reconstruction and wall-expansion projects.
- Tourism-aware visitor infrastructure projects when cultural demand strains urban capacity.
- Bounded historical urban snapshots for long-running worlds.
- Additive `world.urbanEvolution` state preserving prior v4.4 saves.

## Pixel Civilization integration

`urban-evolution-pixel-renderer.js` layers living-city state onto the existing v4.4 renderer chain without replacing archaeology, heritage or tourism visuals.

- Growing districts receive denser, taller pixel blocks.
- Declining districts render reduced/faded building mass.
- Ruined districts receive visibly broken structures.
- Rebuilding districts receive scaffold-like construction accents.
- Districts outside the historic walls receive expansion markers.
- Edge/suburban/outer-ring expansion adds an urban-growth boundary around the settlement preview.
- The bridge adds `urbanEvolution` to Worldbuilding settlement profiles, so existing preview consumers automatically receive v4.5 state.

## Worldbuilding Atelier integration

`urban-ui.js` adds a Living City Evolution section directly to the existing Atelier. The selected settlement now exposes:

- expansion stage, sprawl, wall pressure and skyline metrics;
- growing, ruined and rebuilding district counts;
- per-district lifecycle, density, growth, decline, damage and outside-wall status;
- active reconstruction, wall-expansion and visitor-infrastructure projects.

The panel follows the existing selected settlement and refreshes from the same deterministic world state without creating a parallel city model.

## Universal Encyclopedia integration

`urban-wiki-adapter.js` extends the existing Living Wiki chain with three searchable entity families:

- `urban_district` — lifecycle, role, density, growth, decline, damage and wall status for each modeled district;
- `urban_expansion` — city-level sprawl, expansion stage, wall pressure, skyline and linked districts/projects;
- `urban_project` — reconstruction, wall expansion and visitor-infrastructure works with status and progress.

Urban entries participate in global search, related-entry traversal, dashboard counts, validation and Markdown encyclopedia export. Expansion entries link to their districts and projects, allowing a city’s physical evolution to be followed as a connected knowledge graph rather than isolated UI metrics.

## Architecture

The `urban-evolution-engine.js` layer builds on the existing City Engine and Worldbuilding Engine and optionally consumes the v4.4 Tourism layer. It does not replace existing city generation, businesses, gangs or district layouts. Instead it interprets those systems as a living historical urban fabric.

The engine exposes:

- `initialize(world, force)`
- `simulateUrbanYear(world, year)`
- `settlementView(world, settlementId)`
- `summary(world)`
- `validate(world)`

The standard worldbuilding loader bootstraps the urban engine, v4.5 pixel overlay, encyclopedia adapter and Atelier integration after the v4.4 tourism renderer, preserving extension order.

## Compatibility

v4.5 is additive. Existing v4.4 tourism, heritage, archaeology and worldbuilding state remain valid. Worlds without tourism data still receive urban evolution using city, history and prosperity inputs.
