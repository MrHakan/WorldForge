# WorldForge v2.3 — Households, Social Classes & Demographic Life

WorldForge v2.3 extends the v2.2 labor economy below the settlement aggregate and models how employment, prices, food security, war, institutions and education affect households over time.

## Household economy

Every settlement receives a persistent household profile derived from its live labor market and population. The profile tracks household count, average size, dependents, employed adults, mean income, food and housing costs, disposable income, savings, debt and poverty pressure. A bounded set of deterministic representative households is retained for inspection without storing every family in the world.

## Social classes

The v2.3 class model contains eight strata: Landless Poor, Laborers, Artisans, Merchants, Professionals, Clergy, Minor Nobility and Aristocracy. Class shares are generated from actual local employment sectors, unemployment, settlement prosperity, urbanization and elite concentration. Annual class mobility measures how much the settlement distribution changes between simulation steps.

## Demographic life

Every settlement records fertility, mortality, estimated births and deaths, natural change and life expectancy. Food security, poverty, hospitals, prosperity, recruitment pressure and siege conditions all influence demographic outcomes.

The History engine remains the owner of the world's long-run population curve. To avoid double-counting population growth, v2.3 applies only a bounded demographic correction when local birth/death conditions diverge from a neutral baseline.

## Education and skill pipeline

Libraries, universities and temple archives raise an education index and literacy rate. The household layer also estimates apprentices, journeymen, masters and high-skill professionals. Education feeds back into the v2.2 labor market by gradually improving local skill indices rather than replacing the existing profession and vacancy model.

## Universal Encyclopedia

v2.3 adds three first-class article types:

- `Social Class`
- `Household Profile`
- `Demography`

These link back to settlements and v2.2 labor markets.

## Browser workspace

The Household Register displays world KPIs, settlement household-economy cards, class distribution, demographic indicators, education pipeline counts and a standalone Household Ledger JSON export.

## Runtime integration

`household-loader.js` is chained from the existing v2.2 workforce loader, so deployed GitHub Pages builds retain the established loader architecture while v2.3 loads only after the calibrated workforce layer is ready.

## Regression gate

`.github/workflows/v23-households.yml` validates syntax, required runtime files and the deterministic v2.3 regression. The test verifies settlement coverage, class accounting, representative household generation, fertility/mortality, education cohorts, Universal Encyclopedia integration, chunk-independent simulation and save/export round trips.
