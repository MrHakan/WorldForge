# WorldForge v4.8 — Regional Economy & Logistics

v4.8 turns prosperity into an explicit regional economy with production, demand, storage, prices, trade capacity and supply disruption.

## Completed

- Goods: grain, timber, stone, iron, textiles, luxuries
- Settlement production and demand profiles
- Per-good balances, shortages and surpluses
- Warehouse, dock and caravan capacity
- Logistics throughput from roads, markets, traffic and water access
- Deterministic road, river and sea trade routes
- Route capacity, disruption and blocked-route states
- Import/export flows applied back to settlement balances
- Per-good stockpiles
- Dynamic per-good price pressure and city price index
- Food security and famine-risk indicators
- Economy & Logistics workspace page
- Selected-settlement route inspection
- Trade-route map overlay with road/river/sea and goods filters
- Responsive workspace layering fix so the map does not cover active pages
- CI syntax and contract validation

## v4.8 extension hooks

- `setRouteDisruption(world, routeId, severity)` can model war, blockade, piracy or disaster disruption.
- `clearRouteDisruption(world, routeId)` restores the route.
- Food security and famine risk react to grain balance, imports and stored reserves.
