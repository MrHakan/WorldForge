# WorldForge v4.8 — Regional Economy & Logistics

v4.8 turns city prosperity into explicit production, demand, shortages, surpluses, price pressure and trade flows.

## Implemented

- Goods: grain, timber, stone, iron, textiles, luxuries
- Settlement production profiles influenced by biome, prosperity and geography
- Settlement demand influenced by population, prosperity and infrastructure strain
- Per-good balance, shortage, surplus and price pressure
- Aggregate price index per settlement
- Logistics throughput tied to roads, market flow, traffic and transport capacity
- Warehouse capacity
- Dock capacity
- Caravan capacity
- Deterministic road, river and sea trade routes between surplus and deficit cities
- Route capacity affects actual trade volume
- Economy & Logistics workspace page
- Per-good production/demand/price cards
- Import/export route inspection for the selected settlement
- Lightweight world-map trade-route overlay
- Annual economy summary retained in world state
- CI syntax and contract validation
