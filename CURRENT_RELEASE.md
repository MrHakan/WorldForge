# Current WorldForge Release

## v4.4 — Tourism, Pilgrimage & Cultural Economy

The current release converts the v4.3 cultural-heritage network into a bounded visitor economy. Heritage sites, museums and significant relics attract travelers and pilgrims, settlements develop accommodation capacity and festivals, cultural routes connect destinations, and visitor spending supports both local income and conservation while excessive demand can create overtourism pressure.

Core systems: deterministic cultural destinations; annual visitor and pilgrim flows; visitor-spending, local-income and conservation-share modeling; pilgrimage, museum and heritage routes; heritage festivals and pilgrimage feasts; aggregate inn/lodging capacity, occupancy, jobs and revenue; four-stage visitor pressure from low to overtourism; crowding and vandalism incidents; crisis-driven attractiveness penalties; visitor and lodging Pixel Civilization overlays; Tourism, Pilgrimage & Cultural Economy cards in the Worldbuilding Atelier; tourism ledger export; `tourism_destination`, `pilgrimage_route`, `heritage_festival`, `tourism_accommodation` and `tourism_incident` Universal Encyclopedia types; additive `world.tourism` state; v4.3 heritage compatibility; deterministic save/export reconstruction; and dedicated v4.4 regression coverage.

See [`V4.4.md`](V4.4.md) for complete release notes and architecture details.
