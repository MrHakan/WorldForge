# WorldForge v4.6 — Urban Infrastructure & City Services

v4.6 adds deterministic urban infrastructure on top of v4.5 Living Cities.

## Included

- Road quality and street capacity.
- Water-service capacity.
- Structural fire-risk modeling.
- Market-logistics capacity.
- Gate-capacity pressure.
- Bridge demand for river settlements.
- Aggregate service strain.
- Road, waterworks, bridge and gate projects.
- High-fire-risk incident records.
- Additive urban infrastructure state and annual history.

## Pixel Civilization

The new infrastructure pixel overlay adds visual cues for roads, waterworks, bridge demand, gate pressure, fire risk and service strain while preserving the existing renderer chain.

## Worldbuilding Atelier

The selected settlement now exposes roads, water, fire risk, market flow, gate capacity, bridge demand, service strain and active infrastructure projects.

## Compatibility

v4.6 builds on v4.5 urban evolution and does not replace the existing district lifecycle model.
