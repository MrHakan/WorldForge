# WorldForge v4.4 — Tourism, Pilgrimage & Cultural Economy

WorldForge v4.4 converts the heritage prestige created by v4.2 and v4.3 into a bounded visitor economy. Registered sites, museums and significant relics now attract travelers and pilgrims, create lodging demand and cultural events, generate local income and conservation funding, and can also produce crowding or vandalism pressure.

## Cultural destinations

Every eligible v4.3 heritage property receives a deterministic tourism profile derived from heritage status, heritage score, public access, local museums, displayed relics, settlement importance and recent crisis history.

Destinations track:

- cultural type (`heritage`, `museum`, `pilgrimage`, `festival` or `mixed`);
- attractiveness;
- annual visitors and pilgrim counts;
- modeled visitor spending;
- local-income share;
- conservation contribution;
- carrying capacity;
- visitor pressure;
- resident sentiment;
- overnight-stay share.

Recent battles, sieges, raids and epidemic-like events can reduce current attractiveness, allowing tourism to collapse temporarily when a destination becomes unsafe.

## Pilgrimage

Sacred ruins, temples, shrines, sanctuaries and similar sites can become pilgrimage destinations. Pilgrim flows are a subset of total visitors and can generate dedicated pilgrimage routes and seasonal pilgrimage feasts.

This remains a synthetic WorldForge simulation layer: it models the movement and economic impact of culturally or religiously motivated visitors without representing any real-world religion.

## Cultural routes

Multiple destinations within a realm can be grouped into bounded deterministic routes:

- heritage circuits;
- pilgrimage routes;
- museum routes;
- festival routes.

Routes track linked settlements, annual travelers, route-linked revenue and seasonality.

## Festivals

Attractive destinations can generate heritage festivals or pilgrimage feasts with:

- season;
- attendance;
- economic lift;
- public support;
- crowd risk.

High festival attendance can produce visitor-pressure incidents.

## Accommodation economy

Settlements with visitor demand gain a lightweight accommodation profile containing:

- inn/lodging clusters;
- visitor bed capacity;
- occupancy;
- tourism jobs;
- lodging revenue;
- local capacity pressure.

The model is intentionally aggregate rather than an unbounded hotel simulation.

## Overtourism and conservation pressure

Each destination compares annual visitor demand against modeled carrying capacity. Four pressure states are supported:

1. **Low**
2. **Balanced**
3. **Busy**
4. **Overtourism**

Busy and overtourism destinations can generate crowding and vandalism incidents. Revenue simultaneously supports local income and a deterministic conservation share, creating a visible tradeoff between commercialization and preservation.

## Pixel Civilization

v4.4 adds a tourism layer on top of the existing v4.3 heritage renderer:

- visitor/pilgrim figures appear around tourism-active settlements;
- lodging/inn structures visualize overnight demand;
- overtourism destinations receive a pressure marker;
- existing architecture, ruins, museums and heritage markers remain intact.

## Worldbuilding Atelier

The Atelier gains a **Tourism, Pilgrimage & Cultural Economy** section with:

- global visitor, pilgrim and revenue KPIs;
- destination cards;
- accommodation economy cards;
- cultural-route and pilgrimage-route cards;
- festival records;
- visitor-pressure incidents;
- tourism ledger JSON export.

## Universal Encyclopedia

v4.4 adds five record types:

- `tourism_destination`
- `pilgrimage_route`
- `heritage_festival`
- `tourism_accommodation`
- `tourism_incident`

These cross-link back to heritage sites, cultural routes and local visitor infrastructure.

## State and compatibility

The layer is additive under `world.tourism` with its own version/schema marker. It does not replace or mutate `world.heritage`, `world.archaeology` or `world.worldbuilding`.

The v4.4 CI gate reruns v4.0, v4.1, v4.1.1, v4.2 and v4.3 regression suites before the tourism regression. Dedicated v4.4 coverage validates visitor/revenue generation, pilgrimage detection, pressure states, Pixel Civilization metadata, Encyclopedia records, deterministic save/export reconstruction and preservation of the v4.3 heritage/worldbuilding fingerprints.
