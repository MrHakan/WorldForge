# v5.2.0 — Terrain Atlas

WorldForge now opens as a map-first worldbuilding workspace. Natural terrain replaces the solid political-color grid, and analytical controls no longer cover the canvas.

## The new atlas

- A cached Canvas 2.5D surface uses bilinear elevation sampling, oblique projection and displaced terrain geometry.
- Directional hill shading, coastal shallows, beaches, forests, highland colors and snow make the landscape readable.
- Small settlement buildings and capital flags replace the field of generic circles. Place labels are limited by zoom level and collision checks.
- 2.5D and Flat views use the same projection for drawing, inverse picking, pan, zoom and settlement selection. Pointer cancellation and pinch zoom are handled explicitly.
- Terrain is the default theme. Political, biome, elevation, climate, population and economic analysis remain available through one theme selector.
- The layer drawer starts closed. Trade, military, supply, workforce and strategic overlays are opt-in.
- SVG overlays use the canvas viewport, camera and actual settlement coordinates; missing coordinates no longer produce a fabricated circular layout. Overlays follow camera changes and do not display present-day operational data over historical views.
- Clicking a settlement opens a compact contextual card with a route to its details.

## A quieter workspace

A navigation rail replaces the crowded section row. The secondary navigation remains a small page strip, details live in one scrollable inspector, and the map keeps the remaining space. The inspector can be expanded or hidden to focus on the atlas.

The existing control elements are relocated into the layer drawer rather than duplicated. Advanced economic and operational tools remain available on demand. On smaller screens, navigation becomes an icon rail and details open over the atlas with a visible close button. Portrait maps open on a readable region of inhabited terrain; drag to explore the rest. Import and export remain accessible through the Files menu.

Workspace classification supports both direct panels and inspector panels. Existing feature workspaces, save/import/export controls and the deterministic simulation remain available; this is a presentation release and does not change world schemas or simulation rules.

## Performance and validation

Terrain surfaces are cached outside saved world data, reused during camera movement, and kept in a bounded per-world cache. Natural terrain can be reused across historical years. Strategic indexing is skipped when its overlays are disabled.

Validation includes the existing regression suite, terrain sampling and inverse projection, cache reuse, color parsing, unchanged world fingerprints, real settlement coordinate lookup, browser terrain coverage, projected marker selection, view switching, layer defaults, inspector focus, desktop layout and mobile access.

The Pages pipeline generates desktop and mobile review images in GitHub Actions, retains browser diagnostics, deploys the validated artifact, and publishes the release after deployment succeeds.
