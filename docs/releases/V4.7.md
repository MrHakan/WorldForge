# WorldForge v4.7 — Living Infrastructure & City Activity

## Scope

v4.7 turns urban infrastructure from a mostly statistical layer into visible city activity.

### City activity engine

- Deterministic street activity per settlement
- Workers and carts derived from population, trade, prosperity and infrastructure quality
- Port activity and boat counts for coastal and river settlements
- Traffic pressure tied to roads, gates, bridges and sprawl
- Maintenance intensity tied to service strain, water, roads and fire risk
- Rare prosperity-driven festivals
- Night-light intensity for more developed settlements
- Bounded annual summary history

### Pixel Civilization integration

- Worker and cart activity markers
- Port boats
- Maintenance/construction zones
- Festival decorations
- Settlement night-light accents

### Compatibility

v4.7 is additive and layers on top of v4.5 Urban Evolution and v4.6 Urban Infrastructure. Existing save-state systems remain intact.

## Next targets

- Time-based movement/animation for carts and workers
- Functional docks, warehouses and shipyard activity
- Road wear and road-class evolution
- Localized fire incidents and recovery
- Rebuilding scaffolds and abandoned-quarter vegetation
