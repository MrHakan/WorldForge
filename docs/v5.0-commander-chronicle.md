# v5.0 Commander Battle Chronicle

This milestone extends the existing commander wounds, battlefield death, retirement, succession and legacy systems with a persistent historical chronicle.

## Added

- Links each current-year warfare battle to the commanders of the attacking and defending armies.
- Preserves a fallen commander as the battle participant when death and succession happen in the same simulation year.
- Records battle location, outcome and casualties alongside commander identities.
- Records wounds, battlefield deaths, retirements and succession as career events.
- Keeps chronicle records idempotent by stable event IDs, so read-only UI rerenders cannot duplicate history.
- Caps the persistent chronicle at 400 events.
- Adds `namedBattles` and `chronicleEvents` statistics.
- Adds a read-only Commander Chronicle section to Strategic AI UI.
- Dynamically registers the chronicle at lifecycle order 85 when the module loads, placing it after commander career resolution and before legacy/history consumers on subsequent annual simulations.

## Historical pipeline

`battle -> commander chronicle -> commander legacy -> battlefield history -> relics -> collections -> excavation -> relic journeys -> national memory`

This gives future archaeology and heritage milestones a stable commander/battle provenance source rather than inferring named historical figures from aggregate battle counts.

## Regression contract

`tests/warfare-commander-chronicle-v50.mjs` verifies commander attribution, fallen-commander attribution, wounds, succession and same-year idempotency.
